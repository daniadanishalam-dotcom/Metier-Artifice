Privacy Policy

How Metier Artifice handles information when you browse or contact the studio.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information this website uses and what happens when you contact Dania about resin art, website design, or digital services.

Information you choose to send

The custom-order forms prepare an email in your own email application. The information you enter is not sent to this website. It is sent to Metier Artifice only if you choose to send the email. Messages are received at metierstudio@gmail.com.

The newsletter form also prepares an email draft containing your email address and subscription request. You are not subscribed unless you choose to send that email. This website does not store newsletter addresses or send newsletters automatically. If you send the request, Metier Artifice will use your address for the daily studio updates and newsletters you agreed to receive; email metierstudio@gmail.com to unsubscribe.

If you contact the studio by phone, email, or Instagram, the information you share is handled through the service you use. Please avoid including sensitive personal or financial information in an inquiry.

Shopping cart

Your cart is saved in this browser using local storage so it remains available while you browse. The cart data includes product identifiers, names, prices, and quantities. It is stored on your device and is not sent to a Metier Artifice server by this site. You can remove it by clearing this site's stored data in your browser.

Payments and accounts

This website does not currently provide online checkout, process card payments, or create customer accounts. Order details are arranged directly with the studio.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve those font files, and that request may include technical information such as your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Links to Instagram and your email or phone applications also take you to services governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

The site does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. The shopping cart uses browser local storage as described above; it is not a cookie.

How to contact us

For questions about this policy or to request an update or deletion of information you have emailed, contact metierstudio@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the website's features or information practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of the policy.
