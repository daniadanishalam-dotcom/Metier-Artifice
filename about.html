The maker behind the work

After Every Setback, I Begin Again

A young entrepreneur expanding Metier Artifice through digital services alongside her handcrafted resin art.

Hi, I’m Dania!

My journey with Metier Artifice has taught me that building your dreams isn’t always as easy as it looks. I’ve faced setbacks, self-doubt, difficult days and moments when I questioned my own abilities. Balancing studies, business and personal challenges hasn’t been easy, but I’ve learned that I don’t have to have everything figured out to keep moving forward.

I want every girl who’s struggling, starting over or feeling behind in life to know that your difficult days don’t define your future. You can be scared and still be brave, uncertain and still be ambitious, and face setbacks without giving up on yourself.

I’m still learning, growing and building my dreams, and I’m confident that I’ll find my way through every challenge. If there’s one thing I hope to inspire, it’s this: believe in yourself, give yourself time and never stop creating the life you want.
