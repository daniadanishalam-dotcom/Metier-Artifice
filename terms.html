Flower preservation

Please follow the preparation guidance on the Flower Preservation page and wait for confirmation before mailing your flowers. Flowers must be fully dry and clean. Natural flowers can change in color, shape, or texture during drying and while being set in resin, and results cannot be guaranteed. Flowers that arrive damp, damaged, or unsuitable may not be usable. You are responsible for packing and shipping your flowers safely to the address provided by the studio.
